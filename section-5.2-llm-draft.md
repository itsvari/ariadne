### **5.2 LLM**

The LLM facilitator runs as a stand-alone orchestration service that the Unity client connects to over a bidirectional WebSocket. The Unity client handles rendering, locomotion, and peer-to-peer voice chat; the service handles speech recognition, speech synthesis, model inference, and session state. All participants' headsets connect to the same service, so the LLM operates on a single conversation history across all players.

#### **5.2.1 Dual-Loop Architecture**

Ariadne uses two cooperating language model loops sharing a persistent belief store. A *Talker* (`gpt-oss-120b`, served via Cerebras for low-latency inference) is invoked on every player utterance and decides whether and how Ariadne speaks. A *Thinker* (`qwen/qwen3.6-plus`, served via OpenRouter) runs on a slower scheduled cadence (60 seconds by default) and analyses the full conversation history and current world state. The Thinker maintains a structured *belief store* containing per-participant attributes such as speaking frequency and engagement, the current discussion topic, a periodically compacted conversation summary, and a queue of *directives* (short instructions such as "invite the quieter participant to share their interpretation of the second pillar") for the Talker to consider on subsequent turns. The belief store is persisted in a Valkey instance, written by the Thinker and read by the Talker, letting the two loops run on independent schedules.

#### **5.2.2 Speech Pipeline**

Player audio captured through the Unity client's Vivox microphone tap is segmented locally by voice activity detection and posted to the server, where Deepgram Flux performs streaming transcription with end-of-turn detection. Transcripts are tagged with the originating speaker so the Talker can disambiguate concurrent speakers via a `[speaker]: text` prefix in its conversation context. Ariadne's responses are synthesised through Deepgram Aura 2 and streamed back to the headset as 24 kHz Linear16 PCM, played through a spatial AudioSource attached to its avatar. The TTS stream supports barge-in and is cancelled as soon as a player begins to speak.

#### **5.2.3 Tool-Mediated Behaviour**

Both loops act on the world through structured tool calls. The Talker is given six tools: `speak`, `avatar_command` (covering gaze, gesture, expression, navigation, and idle), `dequeue_directive`, `read_belief_store`, and two coordination tools (`trigger_reasoner` and `wait_for_reasoner`) that let the Talker invoke the Thinker mid-turn when it needs strategic input. The Thinker is given complementary tools to write the belief store, enqueue directives, query world state, compact history, and issue its own planned avatar commands. Each avatar command carries a `reactive` or `planned` priority and is arbitrated by a hold-window scheduler on the Unity side, so that an in-flight gaze toward a quiet participant is not pre-empted by a lower-priority background gesture.

#### **5.2.4 Intervention Policy and World Grounding**

Ariadne is conservative about when to intervene. The Talker's system prompt instructs it to default to silence and emit an `idle` avatar command, speaking only when a player has directly addressed it or when there is a significant participation imbalance, in which case it may gently invite the quieter participants to share their thoughts. The intent is to keep an over-eager facilitator from crowding out the conversation among players. To ground these decisions in events outside the conversational channel, the Unity client publishes a world-state snapshot every 1.5 seconds (and immediately on key events) containing each participant's location, proximity, current activity, and voice activity, alongside puzzle-level events such as `room1_keys:updated` and `room2_combo:solved` reported by the room managers. This snapshot is injected into both contexts as a system message, so that facilitation can be conditioned on, for example, which participants are colocated with which puzzle and which puzzles have already been solved.
